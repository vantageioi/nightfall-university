# Nightfall Brand Voice

## Positioning

**Nightfall is the calm, intelligent companion for a student’s university-application journey.** It turns a scattered, high-stakes process into a clear personal path—applications, documents, deadlines, and next steps held in one place.

Nightfall speaks to students first and reassures families second. It understands that an application is not an operational ticket. It is a sequence of meaningful choices, each made under pressure and each connected to a future the student cares about.

## Brand Character

| Attribute | What it means in practice | What it avoids |
|---|---|---|
| **Warmly capable** | Reassure students with clear next steps and visible progress. | Cold institutional language or fake cheerleading. |
| **Quietly ambitious** | Honor the future the student is building without making promises. | Outcome guarantees, prestige obsession, or hype. |
| **Plainspoken** | Use human words for complex work: “Your next step,” “Ready to review,” “Due in 8 days.” | Acronyms, process jargon, and business-operations vocabulary. |
| **Respectfully bilingual** | Arabic should feel native, warm, and direct—never mechanically translated. | Literal phrasing or a mismatched English interface in RTL. |

## Voice Principles

Start with the student’s position in the journey, then name the next step. “You have two applications ready to review” is better than “Two records require review.” “Your transcript is nearly ready” is better than “Document verification is incomplete.”

Be precise without becoming clinical. Write **“Due Friday, 22 August”**, not “Urgent.” Write **“We found one missing file”**, not “Action required.” Never claim admission, scholarships, or an outcome that Nightfall cannot control.

Make AI supportive and transparent. The preferred framing is: **“Nightfall can organize your work, notice what changed, and help you prepare. You decide what happens next.”**

## Arabic Voice

Nightfall’s Arabic is clear, contemporary, and family-friendly. It uses direct Shami-friendly phrasing where warmth helps, while keeping important status, eligibility, and deadline language in clean professional Arabic.

> **كل خطوة بتفرق. نايتفول بتخلي الخطوة الجاية أوضح.**

Prefer **“خطوتك الجاية”** for “your next step,” **“جاهز للمراجعة”** for “ready to review,” and **“آخر موعد”** for “deadline.” Avoid literal translations of SaaS vocabulary such as “لوحة التحكّم” when a simpler route or progress view makes more sense.

## Message Architecture

| Context | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Landing headline | **Your future has a path.** | **مستقبلك إلو طريق.** |
| Landing support | Every application, document, and deadline—held together with a calmer next step. | كل طلب، كل ورقة، وكل موعد—مرتبين عشان الخطوة الجاية تكون أهدى. |
| Waitlist | **Be first when your journey opens.** | **كون من أول الناس لما تفتح رحلتك.** |
| Onboarding | **Tell us where you’re headed.** | **خبرنا لوين رايح.** |
| Progress | **You’re closer than you think.** | **إنت أقرب مما بتتخيّل.** |
| AI support | **Prepared for you. Always yours to decide.** | **مجهّز إلك. والقرار إلك.** |

## Product Terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| journey, path, next step, application, university list | pipeline, portfolio, working set, CRM |
| your progress, your documents, your dates | operational condition, evidence ledger, matrix |
| review, prepare, save, send when ready | triage, intervention, escalation, agent action |
| family access | staff role, account owner, team seat |

## Event Demonstration Script

Start on the landing page and say: “Applying should feel ambitious, not overwhelming.” Open the personal journey preview, show a student’s application cards, identify a missing document, and move it to ready. Close on the waitlist: “Nightfall does not decide your future. It gives you a calmer way to build it.”
