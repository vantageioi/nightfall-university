# Nightfall UX Direction

Nightfall should feel like a **private international admissions consultant that has become an operating system**, not a university database with an AI layer.

The intended product progression is: **Consultation → Your Options → Your Journey → Your Applications → Nightfall Keeps Watch**. The interface must consistently answer: **What should I do next?**

## Consultation

The Consultant is the front door. It asks only questions that improve understanding, matching, or feasibility. A student can answer in free text, by selecting a structured choice, or by explicitly saying they do not know yet. Account sign-up happens at the final private-unlock moment, not before the conversation.

## Your options

The first research set contains roughly three source-linked options, not a generic catalogue. Each option must visibly explain the evidence used, state what needs official verification, and invite a student reaction. It is research orientation, never an admission, eligibility, visa, funding, or application decision.

## Your journey

The primary workspace prioritizes the current path, meaningful attention, and one clear next action. Saved options are not applications. An application only begins after a genuine student pursuit signal. Documents, requirements, dates, communications, tasks, and agent activity must remain connected to that application context.

## Nightfall keeps watch

Monitoring should be quiet and only surface meaningful official-source changes, deadline relevance, or university messages that need review. AI may read, summarize, classify, draft, suggest, and prepare; it must not send external mail, commit the student, submit an application, or change a consequential state without explicit student approval.

## Visual language

Use night, graphite, paper, and silver; editorial typography; restrained monospace; hairline rules; sparse status symbols; large whitespace; subtle cartographic references; and precise alignment. Avoid generic SaaS card grids, excessive rounded corners, glass effects, neon, artificial AI ornamentation, and dashboard clutter.
